Our Story

We grew up reading heroes who never looked like us

My brother and I spent our school days trading comics and copying panels into our notebooks. Every hero lived in a faraway city. We kept wondering why none of them lived in ours.

In 2022 we stopped wondering and started building. Astra Comics was our answer: an original Indian universe, the ACU, with its own heroes, villains and streets you would recognise. Shock Master was the first story we finished, and we still remember the night we saw the first printed copy.

Today the two of us still do the work ourselves, from the first sketch to packing your order.

  • Original Indian heroes, no borrowed characters
  • One connected universe, so every issue adds to the last
  • Written and drawn by the founders themselves
